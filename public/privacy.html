Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At Nexus Arcade, accessible from nexusarcade.example.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by Nexus Arcade and how we use it.

2. Local Storage Usage (No Invasive Tracking)

Unlike platforms that force account creation or track users across the web, Nexus Arcade relies on client-side HTML5 LocalStorage to save your personal preferences on your device:

  • High Scores: We record your local game high scores and leaderboards under keys such as nexus_leaderboard_data.
  • Favorite Games: Your favorited game IDs are stored under nexus_favorites.
  • Star Ratings: Game ratings you submit are recorded under nexus_rating_[gameId].

This data remains stored exclusively within your browser and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies.

3. Third-Party Advertisers & Google AdSense Disclosures

To maintain our gaming portal as a free service, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense or direct brand partners). These third-party ad servers or networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are sent directly to users' browsers.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or Google Ad Settings.

4. Game Sandboxing & Security

All playable HTML5 games are executed inside secure, sandboxed <iframe> environments with strict permission policies (sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-fullscreen allow-forms allow-pointer-lock"). This isolates games from accessing sensitive browser data, webcam/microphone inputs, or top-level navigation.

5. GDPR & CCPA Data Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you are entitled to:

  • The right to access personal information collected.
  • The right to request deletion of any stored personal data.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Since we do not require account registration or store personal identification on central servers, most data is stored entirely in your local browser storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such records.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact us at: privacy@nexusarcade.example.com.
