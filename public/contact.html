Frequently Asked Questions

Are the games on Nexus Arcade genuinely free to play? ▼
Yes! Every game in our catalog is 100% free to play directly in your web browser. There are no paywalls, hidden software downloads, or subscriptions required to enjoy our entire library.
How do I add my own HTML5 game to Nexus Arcade? ▼
Indie game developers can submit their HTML5 games via our Contact Form by selecting "Game Submission". Our catalog is stored in a clean data/games.json file, allowing approved titles to be seamlessly added with custom artwork, descriptions, and iframe embeds.
How does the VIP Membership work? ▼
The VIP Membership gives you a completely ad-free environment, removes all sponsor placeholders, grants golden leaderboard badges, and unlocks early access to newly tested games. You can cancel at any time with 1-click.
Where are high scores and favorite games saved? ▼
High scores and your favorited games are saved directly inside your browser's private local storage. This eliminates the need for annoying registration forms while keeping your records safe on your device.
